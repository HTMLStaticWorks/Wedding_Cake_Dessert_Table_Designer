Structural Precision

The Anatomy of a 5+ Tier Edible Monument

Building a cake that weighs in excess of 100 pounds requires aerospace-level culinary precision, internal titanium supports, and climate-controlled transport.

01

Internal Titanium Core

Food-grade titanium and concealed central dowel system designed to safely support up to 140 lbs without compromising tender sponge crumb.

02

Refrigerated Transit Vault

Dual-zone mobile refrigeration keeping the cake stabilized at exactly 14°C (57°F) during transport to country estates and castles.

03

Live Sugar Petal Pinning

Fragile botanical sugar peonies and roses are individually secured on-site by our lead pastry sculptor hours prior to guest reception.

04

Digital Spirit Leveling

Historic venues often feature uneven antique parquet floors. We calibrate each riser using digital millimeter leveling pedestals.

Concierge Clarifications

Grand Installation FAQs

Everything you need to know about commissioning an architectural cake or full-scale dessert banquet.

How far in advance should we reserve our Grand Installation?

Because we strictly limit our calendar to only one Grand Installation per weekend worldwide to ensure master pastry chef oversight, we recommend booking 6 to 12 months in advance. Peak spring and autumn wedding dates often fill up a year ahead.

How do you manage warm weather and outdoor estate receptions?

Our monumental cakes are built using proprietary structural food-grade titanium doweling and temperature-stabilized Swiss meringue frostings. We transport cakes in dual-zone refrigerated vans and coordinate with venue coordinators for optimal air-conditioned staging until the ceremonial cake cutting.

Do you coordinate directly with our floral designer and event planner?

Yes. Our atelier coordinates with your floral team, lighting technicians, and table rental companies. We match sugar blossom color pantones with live centerpieces, ensure structural load capacity for tables, and calibrate spotlighting angles for maximum visual impact.

Can different tiers feature distinct flavor combinations and dietary options?

Absolutely. In a 5 to 7-tier installation, each tier can highlight a distinct flavor suite. We also provide companion gluten-free or dairy-conscious tiers and curated vegan French macaron towers for guests with special dietary preferences.

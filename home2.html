The Haute Concierge Experience

From concept mood board to hotel room delivery, our private concierge handles every meticulous gifting detail for your high-profile celebration.

1. Dedicated Bridal Stylist

You are paired with a senior gifting stylist who coordinates directly with your wedding planner, florist, and stationer to ensure Pantone color harmony and crest continuity.

2. Custom Tooling & Prototyping

We create custom CNC brass stamping dies for your monogram, blend bespoke fragrance accords, and ship an approval sample box directly to your residence.

3. Global Destination Logistics

Whether your vows take place in Lake Como, Amalfi Coast, Bali, or Aspen, we manage customs documentation and climate-controlled venue staging.
